## Problem Statement
Studies suggest that most retail forex traders lose money in the long run, with poor discipline and weak risk management among the identified causes. Many traders keep no structured record of their trades and do not enforce their own risk limits, so they cannot review their performance with real data.

## Objectives
**Main objective:** Build an Oracle PL/SQL database that records forex trades against real exchange rates, measures trader performance and blocks trades that break risk limits.

**Specific objectives:**
1. Design a normalized (3NF) database for traders, accounts, currency pairs, exchange rates and trades.
2. Calculate performance metrics (win rate, drawdown, risk per trade) with PL/SQL functions and procedures.
3. Enforce risk rules automatically with triggers and record every blocked attempt in an audit log.
4. Present the results in a live analytics dashboard connected to the Oracle database.

## Data Source
- **Exchange rates:** European Central Bank (ECB) euro foreign exchange reference rates, daily CSV time series. https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/index.en.html
- **Trade records:** *FX_Daracoin_Strategy_Tester* by Meisam Malek Mohammadi (Kaggle, 2024). Backtest results of an automated forex strategy generated by the MQL5 strategy tester, in two CSV files: deals (executed trades) and orders (placed orders). https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/meisammalekmohammadi/fx-daracoin-strategy-tester
- **Size:** the deals file has 662 rows and 13 columns (Time, Deal, Symbol, Type, Direction, Volume, Price, Order, Commission, Swap, Profit, Balance, Comment). Each trade is stored as an entry deal ("in") and an exit deal ("out"), which gives about 331 trades.
- **Coverage:** EURUSD, 26 January 2023 to 26 December 2023, starting balance 50,000.
- **Licence/usage:** the Kaggle dataset is released under the MIT licence. The ECB rates are published for information purposes only and are used here for educational purposes.
- **Privacy:** both sources are public and contain no information that identifies real people.

## Innovation
Analytics dashboard built with Power BI, connected live to the Oracle database. It will show the equity curve, win rate, drawdown over time and the number of trades blocked by risk rules. Business rules stay in PL/SQL; the dashboard only displays the data.

## Planned Tables
CURRENCY_PAIRS, EXCHANGE_RATES, TRADERS, ACCOUNTS, STRATEGIES, TRADES, RISK_RULES, AUDIT_LOG

## Planned PL/SQL Features
- **Functions:** position size from risk percentage, profit/loss from entry and exit price (contract size 100,000), win rate, maximum drawdown
- **Procedures:** open a trade, close a trade and calculate profit/loss, load and pair entry/exit deals into trades
- **Cursors:** loop through trades to build a monthly performance report
- **Triggers:** block a trade whose risk exceeds the account's per-trade limit (planned 2%) or that breaks the daily loss limit (planned 3%), and write every blocked attempt to the audit log. Limits are stored in the RISK_RULES table so they can be changed.
- **Package:** group all features in one package (for example FX_TRADING_PKG)
- **Exception handling:** clear error messages for invalid trades

## Limitations
- The trade records are backtest results from one automated strategy and one symbol (EURUSD), not live trades of a human trader.
- ECB rates are daily reference rates and are not tradable prices.
